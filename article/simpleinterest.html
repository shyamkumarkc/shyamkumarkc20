What is Simple Interest?

Simple Interest (SI) is a method of calculating interest where interest is calculated only on the original principal amount for the entire period.

Unlike compound interest, previously earned interest is not added to the principal when calculating subsequent interest.

In simple terms: Simple Interest is interest calculated on the original principal amount only.

Simple interest is commonly used for certain short-term loans, advances, financial calculations, educational examples and specific banking products depending on their terms.

Simple Interest Formula

The standard formula for calculating Simple Interest is:

Simple Interest Formula
SI = (P × R × T) / 100
P = Principal | R = Annual Interest Rate | T = Time in Years

The total amount after adding Simple Interest to the original principal can be calculated using:

Total Amount Formula
A = P + SI
Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

Principal, Rate and Time in Simple Interest

P
Principal

The original amount of money invested, deposited or borrowed.

R
Rate

The annual interest rate expressed as a percentage.

T
Time

The period for which the money remains invested or borrowed.

How to Convert Time into Years

  • 6 months = 0.5 year
  • 3 months = 0.25 year
  • 9 months = 0.75 year
  • 2 years = 2 years

Simple Interest Calculation Example

Example

Suppose a person deposits or lends Rs. 100,000 at an annual interest rate of 10% for 2 years.

P = Rs. 100,000
R = 10%
T = 2 years

SI = (P × R × T) / 100

SI = (100,000 × 10 × 2) / 100

SI = Rs. 20,000

Therefore, the Simple Interest for two years is Rs. 20,000.

Total Amount: Rs. 100,000 + Rs. 20,000 = Rs. 120,000

Advantages and Limitations of Simple Interest

Advantages of Simple Interest

  • Easy to understand and calculate.
  • Requires only principal, rate and time.
  • Useful for short-term financial calculations.
  • Provides predictable interest when the principal, rate and period remain constant.

Limitations of Simple Interest

  • Does not account for interest compounding.
  • May not accurately represent products where interest is compounded.
  • Actual banking products may use different interest calculation conventions.
  • Loan agreements should always be checked for the applicable calculation method.

Important Points to Remember

  • Simple Interest is calculated on the original principal.
  • The standard formula is SI = (P × R × T) / 100 .
  • Annual interest rates generally require time to be expressed in years.
  • Total amount is calculated as A = P + SI .
  • Always check the applicable terms and interest calculation method of an actual banking product.
