ATM: Full Form, Meaning, Types, Services

An Automated Teller Machine (ATM) is an electronic banking terminal that allows customers to perform selected banking transactions without visiting a bank counter. ATMs are widely used for cash withdrawals, balance inquiries and other self-service banking facilities in Nepal and around the world.

ATM Full Form: Automated Teller Machine.
Main Purpose: To provide convenient, self-service access to banking transactions.

1. Meaning and Definition of ATM

ATM stands for Automated Teller Machine. It is an electronic device that connects a customer's banking card with the banking system to perform authorized transactions.

Customers generally authenticate themselves using a card and a Personal Identification Number (PIN). Depending on the machine, bank, account and card network, additional authentication methods or cardless services may also be available.

ATMs reduce dependence on bank counters by making selected services available outside normal banking hours.

2. How Does an ATM Work?

An ATM communicates with the relevant banking system to authenticate the customer and process a requested transaction. A typical cash withdrawal follows these steps:

  1. Insert or tap the supported banking card.
  2. Select the preferred language, if available.
  3. Enter the PIN securely.
  4. Select the cash withdrawal option.
  5. Choose the account type and enter the amount.
  6. The banking system checks the transaction details, available balance and applicable limits.
  7. If approved, the ATM dispenses cash.
  8. Collect the cash, card and receipt, if issued.

The exact process varies by ATM and card issuer. If a transaction fails, the account balance and transaction status should be checked before attempting it again.

3. Types of ATMs

3.1 On-site ATM

An on-site ATM is installed within a bank branch or its premises. It provides convenient access to self-service transactions for customers visiting the branch.

3.2 Off-site ATM

An off-site ATM is located away from a bank branch, such as in shopping centres, airports, business areas and other public locations.

3.3 Cash Dispenser

A cash dispenser primarily provides cash withdrawals and may offer a limited range of additional functions.

3.4 Cash Deposit ATM

A cash deposit ATM accepts cash deposits where the service is supported. Some machines can also accept cheques or provide other account services.

3.5 Recyclable ATM

A cash-recycling ATM can accept eligible deposited notes and dispense suitable notes to customers, subject to the machine's capabilities and bank procedures.

4. Services Provided by ATMs

The services available depend on the bank and ATM network.

  • Cash withdrawal
  • Account balance inquiry
  • Mini statement or recent transaction history
  • PIN change, where supported
  • Cash deposit at compatible machines
  • Funds transfer at participating machines
  • Utility payments or mobile top-ups on supported systems
  • Cheque-related services at compatible machines
  • Receipt printing and other account services

Not every ATM supports all these services. Customers should check their bank's official service information before relying on a particular function.

5. Advantages of ATMs

  • Convenience: Customers can access selected banking services without visiting a teller.
  • Extended access: Many ATMs operate beyond regular branch hours.
  • Time saving: Routine transactions can be completed without waiting at a bank counter.
  • Wider access: ATMs can make cash available in locations away from bank branches.
  • Self-service: Customers can carry out routine transactions independently.
  • Reduced counter workload: ATMs handle certain routine transactions automatically.

6. Disadvantages of ATMs

  • Cash may be unavailable when the machine runs out.
  • Technical failures or network interruptions can prevent transactions.
  • Cards may be retained by a machine following a malfunction or security rule.
  • Fraud, card skimming and PIN theft are possible.
  • Withdrawal limits and service charges may apply.
  • ATMs may not provide the full range of banking services.
  • Some locations may be difficult to access for people with disabilities.

7. ATM Safety Tips

Follow these precautions when using an ATM:

  • Cover the keypad while entering your PIN.
  • Never share your PIN, password or OTP with anyone.
  • Check the card slot and keypad for suspicious devices.
  • Use a well-lit ATM in a secure location whenever possible.
  • Do not accept unsolicited help from strangers.
  • Keep your card and cash secure after completing a transaction.
  • Enable transaction alerts if your bank offers them.
  • Never leave your card or receipt behind.
  • Use your bank's official helpline if your card is retained.
  • If cash is not dispensed but your account is debited, record the location, time and transaction details and report the issue promptly to your bank.
Important: A bank will not need you to disclose your ATM PIN or OTP to investigate a complaint. Do not share these credentials with callers claiming to represent a bank.

8. ATMs in Nepal

ATMs are an important part of Nepal's banking and payment infrastructure. Banks and financial institutions provide ATM services through their own machines and supported payment-card networks.

Customers in Nepal can use eligible cards for cash withdrawals and other supported transactions. Availability, transaction limits and charges depend on the card issuer, the ATM operator, the card network and applicable rules.

Before using an ATM operated by another bank, customers should check their bank's applicable withdrawal limits and service charges. Cross-border card usage may be subject to separate limits and fees.

For the latest applicable rules, refer to the official website of Nepal Rastra Bank and your account-holding bank.

9. ATM Card vs Debit Card

Basis ATM Card Debit Card
Main purpose Access to supported ATM services ATM access and, where enabled, purchases and other payments
Cash withdrawal Usually supported Usually supported
Point-of-sale payments Not necessarily supported Supported where the card and merchant accept the transaction
Online payments Not necessarily supported May be supported if enabled

The terms can overlap in everyday usage. A debit card can often be used at ATMs, while the exact capabilities of any card depend on its issuer and network.

10. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the full form of ATM?

ATM stands for Automated Teller Machine.

What is the main purpose of an ATM?

Its main purpose is to provide self-service access to cash withdrawals and other supported banking transactions.

Can I use an ATM 24 hours a day?

Many ATMs are available 24 hours a day, but access depends on the location, machine condition, cash availability and network status.

What should I do if an ATM deducts money but does not dispense cash?

Keep the transaction details, note the ATM location and time, and contact your bank through its official customer-service channel. Avoid repeating the transaction until you have checked its status.

Are ATM transactions free in Nepal?

Not necessarily. Charges depend on the bank, card, ATM network, transaction type and applicable rules. Check the latest official tariff published by your bank.

What is an ATM PIN?

A Personal Identification Number (PIN) is a security credential used to authenticate certain card transactions. It must be kept confidential.
