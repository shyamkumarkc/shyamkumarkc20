CDS & Clearing Limited (CDSC) Nepal

What is CDSC Nepal?

CDSC, or CDS and Clearing Limited, is Nepal's central securities depository responsible for depository, clearing and settlement services for securities in the capital market.

Introduction to CDSC Nepal

CDS and Clearing Limited, commonly known as CDSC, is a company established to provide centralized depository, clearing and settlement services in Nepal's securities market.

CDSC was promoted by Nepal Stock Exchange Limited (NEPSE) and is responsible for maintaining securities in dematerialized form and facilitating electronic securities transactions.

The depository system allows securities such as equities and other eligible instruments to be held electronically instead of using physical certificates.

Core Functions of CDSC

  • Electronic settlement of securities transactions
  • Dematerialization of physical securities
  • Electronic ownership transfer
  • Safe custody and record keeping of securities
  • Generation and maintenance of ISIN information for eligible securities
  • Clearing and settlement support for securities transactions

CDSC and Demat Accounts

CDSC plays an important role in Nepal's dematerialized securities system. Investors can hold securities electronically through a Demat account maintained through a Depository Participant (DP).

The electronic system reduces dependence on physical share certificates and makes the transfer and settlement of securities more efficient.

ISIN and CDSC

An International Securities Identification Number (ISIN) is a unique identification code assigned to securities. CDSC maintains securities-related information and facilitates identification and electronic processing of eligible securities.

Why Is CDSC Important?

CDSC is an important part of Nepal's capital-market infrastructure. It supports secure and efficient electronic settlement and helps maintain electronic records of securities ownership.

The depository and settlement system also reduces risks associated with physical certificates and facilitates faster processing of securities transactions.

CDSC and Nepal's Capital Market

CDSC works within Nepal's broader capital-market infrastructure alongside institutions such as Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE), Securities Board of Nepal (SEBON), Depository Participants and other market intermediaries.
