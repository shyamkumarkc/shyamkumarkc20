DP Charges in Nepal 2026

DP charges are fees related to Demat and depository services in Nepal. Investors may pay charges for opening and maintaining a Demat account, using MeroShare, transferring securities after a sale and using other depository services.

What is a DP Charge?

DP stands for Depository Participant. A DP is the intermediary through which investors access depository services provided through the CDS and Clearing Limited (CDSC) system.

When an investor opens a Demat account, the investor's securities are maintained in electronic form through the depository system. The DP acts as the investor's point of contact for various depository services.

DP charges are therefore different from brokerage commission, SEBON fees and capital gains tax.

What is the Rs. 25 DP Charge?

The Rs. 25 DP charge is commonly associated with the transfer of securities from an investor's Demat account following an on-market sale.

For example, if an investor sells shares of a listed company through a stock broker, the securities must be transferred from the investor's Demat account for settlement. A DP charge of Rs. 25 is commonly applied to this transfer.

Example: If you sell shares of one company in a settlement transaction, the commonly applicable DP transfer charge is Rs. 25. The charge is separate from brokerage, SEBON regulatory fees and applicable taxes.

Mero Share Charge in Nepal

Mero Share is the online service through which investors can access several CDSC-related services. Investors can view their holdings, transaction information and portfolio and can use services such as EDIS and C-ASBA through the platform.

The commonly published MeroShare annual renewal charge is Rs. 50. The MeroShare fee is separate from the annual Demat account maintenance fee of Rs. 100.

Demat Charge and Mero Share Charge Are Different

Investors sometimes confuse Demat and MeroShare charges. They are separate services.

Item Common Charge Purpose
Demat Account Rs. 100/year Maintaining the securities account.
MeroShare Rs. 50/year Online access to Mero Share services.
DP Transfer Rs. 25 Securities transfer associated with an applicable transaction.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the DP charge in Nepal?

The commonly applicable DP charge for an on-market securities transfer is Rs. 25 per transfer. The exact application may depend on the transaction and applicable DP/CDSC rules.

How much does a Demat account cost in Nepal?

A commonly published Demat account opening fee is Rs. 50, while the annual maintenance fee is Rs. 100.

How much does Mero Share cost?

The commonly published MeroShare annual renewal fee is Rs. 50.

Is the Rs. 25 DP charge paid every time I buy shares?

The commonly applicable Rs. 25 DP charge is associated with an applicable securities transfer, particularly following a market sale. It is not normally a separate Rs. 25 charge simply for purchasing shares.

Is Dematerialization free?

Many current DP tariff schedules list dematerialization as free. Investors should nevertheless confirm the current tariff of their DP.

Is DP charge the same as brokerage?

No. DP charge and brokerage are different. Brokerage is charged for stock trading services, while DP charges relate to applicable depository services and securities transfers.

Does every DP charge exactly the same amount?

Not necessarily for every service. Common charges such as Demat maintenance, MeroShare and market-related securities transfer charges are widely published, while some additional services may have DP-specific tariffs.
