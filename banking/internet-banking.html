Internet Banking

Internet banking, also known as online banking or e-banking, is an electronic banking system that allows customers of banks and financial institutions to access banking services through the internet.

Internet Banking Definition: Internet banking is a service that enables customers to perform various banking and financial transactions electronically through a bank's website without visiting a physical branch.

What is Internet Banking?

Internet banking allows customers to manage their bank accounts using a computer, laptop, tablet or smartphone connected to the internet. Customers can log in to their bank's official internet banking platform and perform different financial and non-financial transactions.

Internet banking has made banking services more convenient by allowing customers to access their accounts from almost anywhere and at any time, subject to the bank's service availability.

Services Available Through Internet Banking

Depending on the bank and type of account, internet banking may provide services such as:

  • Checking account balance
  • Viewing bank statements and transaction history
  • Transferring money between accounts
  • Sending funds to other bank accounts
  • Paying utility bills
  • Making online payments
  • Managing beneficiaries
  • Requesting bank statements
  • Checking loan and deposit information
  • Opening or applying for selected banking products
  • Managing cheque-related services
  • Receiving transaction notifications

Features of Internet Banking

  • 24/7 Access: Customers can access many banking services outside normal branch hours.
  • Online Fund Transfer: Money can be transferred electronically between eligible accounts.
  • Account Information: Customers can view balances and transaction history.
  • Bill Payment: Selected utility and service bills can be paid online.
  • Digital Statements: Customers can access or download account statements.
  • Secure Authentication: Banks generally use passwords, OTPs and other security mechanisms to protect accounts.

Benefits of Internet Banking

1. Convenience

Customers can perform many banking activities online without visiting a bank branch.

2. Saves Time

Online banking reduces the need to stand in queues or visit a branch for routine transactions.

3. Easy Account Monitoring

Customers can check their account balance and transaction history conveniently.

4. Faster Payments and Transfers

Internet banking can make fund transfers and online payments faster and more convenient.

5. Reduced Paperwork

Many banking activities can be completed electronically, reducing the need for physical forms and paper statements.

Internet Banking vs Mobile Banking

Basis Internet Banking Mobile Banking
Platform Usually accessed through a bank's website Usually accessed through a mobile application
Device Computer, laptop, tablet or smartphone Primarily smartphone or tablet
Internet Requires internet connectivity Generally requires internet connectivity
Accessibility Accessible through a web browser Accessible through a banking application

How to Use Internet Banking

  1. Open the official website of your bank.
  2. Select the internet banking or online banking option.
  3. Enter your registered login credentials.
  4. Complete any additional authentication required by the bank.
  5. Select the banking service you want to use.
  6. Confirm the transaction and log out after completing your activities.
Security Tip: Always access internet banking through your bank's official website. Never share your password, PIN, OTP or other authentication credentials with anyone.

Security of Internet Banking

Security is an important part of internet banking. Banks use different security technologies and authentication methods to protect customers and their financial information.

Customers should also follow safe online banking practices, such as using strong passwords, avoiding suspicious links, keeping devices updated and never sharing OTPs or passwords.

Internet Banking in Nepal

Banks and financial institutions in Nepal provide digital banking services that allow customers to perform various banking activities electronically. The exact services, transaction limits, charges and authentication methods vary between institutions.

Customers should check the official website or mobile application of their respective bank for the latest information about available internet banking services and fees.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is internet banking?

Internet banking is an electronic banking service that allows customers to perform banking transactions through the internet.

Is internet banking the same as e-banking?

The terms are often used interchangeably, although e-banking can be used as a broader term for electronic banking services.

Can I transfer money through internet banking?

Yes. Most internet banking platforms provide fund-transfer services, subject to the bank's rules, transaction limits and available services.

Is internet banking safe?

Internet banking can be secure when customers use official banking platforms and follow recommended security practices.

What is required to use internet banking?

Generally, customers need an eligible bank account, internet banking registration, login credentials and an internet-connected device.
