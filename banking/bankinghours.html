Banking Hours in Nepal

Bank branch operating hours and transaction hours are not necessarily identical. A branch may remain open while particular banking transactions have a separate cut-off time.

Customers should therefore check the latest schedule of their bank and branch before visiting, particularly during public holidays, special notices, seasonal schedules or changes issued by the regulator.

Important: Banking hours may change because of public holidays, special regulatory notices, local circumstances or bank branch arrangements. The bank timings shown below are based on the available data in the website's commercial bank.

Banking Services Available Outside Branch Hours

Branches have fixed operating schedules, but many banking services can be accessed electronically outside normal branch hours.

  • Mobile banking
  • Internet banking
  • ATM services
  • Digital fund transfers
  • Card payments
  • Some account and payment services through digital channels

Availability and transaction limits depend on the bank, service, system maintenance and applicable rules.

Banking Hours vs Transaction Cut-off Time

One of the most important points for customers is that branch opening time does not always mean that every transaction can be processed until the branch closes.

For example, cheque clearing, IPS, RTGS and other payment systems may have separate operating sessions and cut-off times. Customers making time-sensitive transactions should therefore check the applicable service schedule.
