Banking Hours & Transaction Timings in Nepal

Banking hours in Nepal can vary according to the bank, branch, banking service, weekday, season and instructions issued by Nepal Rastra Bank. This page provides a practical guide to bank opening hours, transaction timings and commercial banks in Nepal.

Last reviewed: September 2026

Banking Hours in Nepal

Bank branch operating hours and transaction hours are not necessarily identical. A branch may remain open while particular banking transactions have a separate cut-off time.

Customers should therefore check the latest schedule of their bank and branch before visiting, particularly during public holidays, special notices, seasonal schedules or changes issued by the regulator.

Important: Banking hours may change because of public holidays, special regulatory notices, local circumstances or bank branch arrangements. The table on this page is intended as a general reference and should not be treated as a guarantee that every branch operates at exactly the same time.

General Banking Transaction Timings

Sunday–Thursday Regular banking days. Actual branch and transaction hours can vary by bank and service.
Friday Many banking and regulatory services operate for shorter hours on Friday.
Public Holidays Branch services may be closed or limited. Banks may publish special holiday arrangements.

Banking Services Available Outside Branch Hours

Branches have fixed operating schedules, but many banking services can be accessed electronically outside normal branch hours.

  • Mobile banking
  • Internet banking
  • ATM services
  • Digital fund transfers
  • Card payments
  • Some account and payment services through digital channels

Availability and transaction limits depend on the bank, service, system maintenance and applicable rules.

Banking Hours vs Transaction Cut-off Time

One of the most important points for customers is that branch opening time does not always mean that every transaction can be processed until the branch closes.

For example, cheque clearing, IPS, RTGS and other payment systems may have separate operating sessions and cut-off times. Customers making time-sensitive transactions should therefore check the applicable service schedule.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the normal banking hours in Nepal?

Banking hours depend on the bank, branch, service and applicable regulatory schedule. Customers should check their specific bank or branch for the latest hours.

Are banks open on Friday in Nepal?

Friday hours can be shorter than regular banking days. The exact schedule may depend on the bank and current regulatory instructions.

Can I use mobile banking after bank closing time?

Many mobile banking services remain available outside branch hours, although some transactions may be subject to limits, maintenance periods or service-specific cut-off times.

Do all commercial banks have the same banking hours?

No. Bank and branch operating schedules can differ. Customers should confirm the operating hours of the particular branch they plan to visit.
