Frequently Asked Questions About Paid-Up Capital of BFIs

1. What is paid-up capital in a bank?

Paid-up capital is the amount of share capital that shareholders have actually paid to a company in exchange for its shares.

2. What is the minimum paid-up capital requirement for BFIs in Nepal?

The minimum paid-up capital requirement varies according to the category, class and operating level of a bank or financial institution. Refer to the requirement table above for the available breakdown.

3. What is the difference between minimum capital and actual paid-up capital?

Minimum capital is the regulatory threshold an institution is required to maintain, whereas actual paid-up capital represents the capital paid by its shareholders.

4. Do commercial banks and development banks have the same capital requirement?

Capital requirements can differ according to the institution's category and applicable regulatory provisions.

5. Where can I compare the paid-up capital of individual BFIs?

You can use the category tabs above to compare the paid-up capital information of commercial banks, development banks, finance companies and microfinance institutions.

Note: Capital requirements and actual paid-up capital figures may change over time. Readers should consult the latest applicable Nepal Rastra Bank directives and official institutional disclosures when making regulatory or financial decisions.
