Mobile Banking in Nepal: Services, Features and Benefits

Mobile banking is a financial service provided by banks and other financial institutions that allows customers to conduct financial transactions remotely using a mobile device such as a smartphone or tablet.

Financial institutions generally provide mobile banking services through their own mobile applications, which are usually available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Transaction limits and transaction amounts may apply according to the rules and directives issued by the central bank and the respective financial institution.

Through a mobile banking application, customers can generally check their account balance, obtain account statements, transfer funds, make electronic bill payments, make QR payments and access various other banking services. The services available through mobile banking may differ from one financial institution to another.

General Services Provided Through Mobile Banking

The following are some of the common services that banks and financial institutions provide through their mobile banking applications:

  1. Check account balance.
  2. View and download account statements.
  3. Electronic fund transfer and bill payments.
  4. QR payments and merchant payments.
  5. Apply for fixed deposits.
  6. Apply for loans against fixed deposits, where available.
  7. Pay utility bills and other digital services.
  8. Transfer funds to other bank accounts.

Benefits of Mobile Banking

Mobile banking provides convenient access to banking services without requiring customers to visit a bank branch. It can save time, provide access to banking services outside regular branch hours and make everyday financial transactions easier.
