What Is Mobile Banking?

Mobile banking is a digital banking service that enables customers to access their bank accounts and perform selected banking transactions through a mobile phone or other supported mobile device.

Banks generally provide mobile banking through an official mobile application. Customers can use the application to access banking services without physically visiting a branch, subject to the bank's terms, transaction limits and service availability.

Mobile Banking in Nepal

Mobile banking is an important part of digital banking in Nepal. Commercial banks and other financial institutions provide mobile applications that allow customers to perform a range of banking and payment-related activities using smartphones.

The exact services available through mobile banking depend on the respective financial institution. Some applications provide basic account information and fund transfers, while others offer additional services such as QR payments, utility bill payments, mobile recharge, fixed deposit services and card-related features.

Benefits of Mobile Banking

Mobile banking provides convenient access to many banking services without requiring customers to visit a branch for every transaction.

1. Convenient Banking

Customers can access supported banking services using a mobile device, reducing the need for routine branch visits.

2. Faster Transactions

Fund transfers, payments and other supported transactions can generally be completed digitally.

3. Easy Access to Account Information

Customers can check balances and transaction information through their banking application.

4. Digital Payments

Mobile banking applications can support QR payments, bill payments, mobile recharge and other digital payment services.

5. Reduced Branch Visits

Many routine banking activities can be performed digitally, potentially reducing the need to visit a physical branch.

How to Activate Mobile Banking in Nepal

The mobile banking registration and activation process differs from one bank to another. A general process may include:

  1. Maintain an eligible account with the bank.
  2. Register for the bank's mobile banking service.
  3. Provide the required customer information.
  4. Install the official mobile banking application.
  5. Complete the activation or verification process.
  6. Create the required login credentials or transaction PIN.
  7. Start using the services available through the application.

Customers should follow the registration instructions provided by their respective bank because the exact process may vary.

Mobile Banking Charges and Transaction Limits

Mobile banking charges and transaction limits can vary according to the bank, transaction type and applicable rules. Some services may be free while charges may apply to particular transactions or services.

Because banking fees and transaction limits can change, customers should check the latest schedule of charges and transaction limits published by their respective bank before making a transaction.

Mobile Banking Security Tips

Customers should follow basic security practices when using mobile banking applications.

  • Use the official mobile banking application provided by your bank.
  • Never share your OTP, password, MPIN or transaction PIN.
  • Do not install banking applications from unknown sources.
  • Keep your smartphone's operating system and banking application updated.
  • Use a strong screen lock on your mobile device.
  • Avoid clicking suspicious banking links received through unknown messages.
  • Contact your bank immediately if you notice an unauthorized transaction.
  • Do not provide confidential banking information to unknown callers.

Frequently Asked Questions About Mobile Banking

What is mobile banking?

Mobile banking is a digital banking service that allows customers to access and perform selected banking transactions through a mobile device.

What services can be used through mobile banking?

Depending on the bank, customers may be able to check account balances, view statements, transfer funds, pay bills, make QR payments, recharge mobile phones and access other banking services.

Is mobile banking available 24 hours a day?

Many mobile banking services can be accessed outside normal branch operating hours. However, particular services may be temporarily unavailable because of maintenance, system issues or other restrictions.

Is mobile banking the same as internet banking?

No. Both provide digital banking services, but mobile banking is generally designed around a mobile application, while internet banking is commonly accessed through a web browser.

Is mobile banking safe?

Mobile banking can be used securely when customers follow their bank's security guidance, use official applications and protect passwords, PINs and OTPs.

Do all banks have the same mobile banking services?

No. Mobile banking features, charges, transaction limits and supported services can differ between banks.

Conclusion

Mobile banking has made banking services more convenient by allowing customers to access many routine services through a mobile device. Account information, fund transfers, QR payments, bill payments and other digital services can be available through a bank's mobile application.

However, services, transaction limits and charges vary between financial institutions. Customers should always check the latest information from their respective bank before using a particular mobile banking service.
