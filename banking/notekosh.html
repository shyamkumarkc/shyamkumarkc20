Note Kosh in Nepal

Note Kosh (नोट कोष), commonly known as a Note Chest or Currency Chest, is a designated currency-management facility used to store, supply, deposit and withdraw Nepalese currency. In Nepal, Note Chests operate as part of the currency-management system of Nepal Rastra Bank (NRB), including through designated banking locations. In the context of Nepal, Note Kosh is typically associated with the Note Department of the Nepal Rastra Bank (NRB), the central bank of Nepal. Note Kosh is a vault maintained in commercial bank by central bank in order to keep Nepalese Currency. It is maintained where there is no branch of NRB for the purpose of helping bank and financial instituions of particular area for easy fund or liquidity management.

The commission rate of Note Kosh is 0.40 % at the time of deposit and 0.20 % at the time of withdrawal, and the transactions can be done in multiple of 1000 only. Nepal Rastra Bank has maintained note kosh in government commercial bank i.e. Rastriya Banijya Bank and Nepal Bank Limited. Among 75 note kosh, 8 note kosh are within Nepal Rastra Bank, 42 are in Rastriya Banijya Bank and 25 are in Nepal Bank Limited. Such note kosh are called "Note Chest" by NRB since they keep physical cash and make necessary supply which are managed by Currency Management Department and handled by designated commercial banks.

Cash deposited in Note Kosh can be taken at the time of calculation of Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR). It handles both newly printed notes and notes withdrawn from circulation (old, torn, or damaged).

Frequently Asked Questions About Note Kosh

What is Note Kosh in Nepal?

Note Kosh is a designated currency-management facility used to support the storage, supply, deposit and withdrawal of Nepalese currency. It is commonly referred to as a Note Chest or Currency Chest.

What is another name for Note Kosh?

Note Kosh is commonly known in English as Note Chest or Currency Chest.

Who manages Note Kosh in Nepal?

Nepal Rastra Bank manages Nepal's currency system. Historically, Note Chests have also been maintained through designated branches of Nepal Bank Limited and Rastriya Banijya Bank.

How many Note Chests are there in Nepal?

Nepal Rastra Bank's historical publication documented 75 Note Chests: 8 under Nepal Rastra Bank, 25 under Nepal Bank Limited and 42 under Rastriya Banijya Bank. This is a historical published figure and should not automatically be interpreted as the current 2026 total.

Is Note Kosh the same as a normal bank vault?

No. A Note Kosh is part of the currency-management arrangement associated with Nepal Rastra Bank, while a normal bank vault primarily provides secure storage for an individual bank's cash and valuables.

Why is Note Kosh important?

Note Kosh is important because it supports the availability, movement and management of physical Nepalese currency across different parts of the country.
