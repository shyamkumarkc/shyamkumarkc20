Frequently Asked Questions

What is a transaction limit?

A transaction limit is the maximum amount that can generally be transferred, withdrawn or paid through a particular banking or payment channel during a specified period.

Are transaction limits the same for every bank in Nepal?

No. Limits may vary depending on the bank, financial institution, card type, payment channel and transaction type.

What do daily and per-transaction limits mean?

A per-transaction limit is the maximum amount allowed in a single transaction, while a daily limit represents the maximum amount allowed over the applicable day.

Can transaction limits change?

Yes. Banks and payment service providers may change transaction limits based on their policies, regulatory requirements and service conditions.

Important: Transaction limits may vary between banks, financial institutions, card issuers and payment channels. Always verify the applicable limit with your bank or payment service provider before making a transaction.
