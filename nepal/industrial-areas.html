Industrial Area Management in Nepal

Industrial Area Management Limited is responsible for the management and operation of industrial areas under the Government of Nepal. Its activities include industrial land management, infrastructure management and support for industries operating within industrial areas.

Management organization: Industrial Area Management Limited (IDML)

Frequently Asked Questions

How many industrial areas are there in Nepal?

Nepal has established industrial areas as well as industrial-area projects in different parts of the country. The number can change as new projects are developed and brought into operation.

What is an industrial area?

An industrial area is a designated location developed with infrastructure and facilities to support manufacturing and other industrial activities.

Which organization manages industrial areas in Nepal?

Industrial Area Management Limited manages established industrial areas under the Government of Nepal.

Which province has industrial areas in Nepal?
