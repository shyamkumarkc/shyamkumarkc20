About Gas Industries in Nepal

Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG) is widely used as a cooking fuel in Nepal. LPG gas industries and bottling plants distribute cooking gas to households, businesses, hotels and other consumers throughout the country.

Gas industries generally receive LPG in bulk, store it safely, fill LPG cylinders and distribute the cylinders through dealers and distributors.

The information on this page can be used to find LPG companies, gas brands and their locations in Nepal.
