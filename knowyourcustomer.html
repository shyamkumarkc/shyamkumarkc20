KNOW YOUR CUSTOMER (KYC)


KYC is the mostly popular in banking and financial institution. KYC describes the process of obtaining, retaining and using information about the customer to identify his/her identity, to verify his/her residential address, sources of funds, wealth is understood and his nature of transaction.

The objective of KYC regulation is to prevent banks and financial institutions from being used intentionally or unintentionally for money laundering and terrorist financing activities.

Importance of KYC

  1. To prevent money laundering
  2. To minimize operational as well as other risks
  3. For better client protection
  4. To maintain better customer relationship
  5. For product design and targeting
  6. To prevent any other banking fraud

KYC Procedure

  • KYC form fill-up
  • Personal interview and observation
  • Collection of supportive documents
  • Classification of customer (Low/Medium/High Risk)
  • Regular Update

NRB guidelines on KYC

  1. Prepare KYC policy including risk management and monitoring process
  2. Customer identification
  3. Customer acceptance
  4. Ceiling of cash transactions
  5. Monitoring of Cash transactions
  6. Risk Management
  7. Monitoring procedures
  8. Verification of the submitted document
  9. Prevention of records up to 5 years
  10. Periodic reporting and loan disclosure
  11. Submit transaction to NRB

Legal Provisions for KYC

Customers to be identified

Any Bank & Financial Institution or Non Financial Institution shall maintain clear records of identity of a person while establishing any kind of relationship with such person or while transacting amount above threshold either in a single transaction or in series of transaction as prescribed by NRB from time to time by publishing a notice.

BFI while indentifying customer shall submit documents as follows:

  1. Incase of natural person name, surname, copy of citizenship or passport including other necessary documents that substantiate his/her permanent residential address and profession or business.
  2. In case of person or firm except those provided in clause (a), copy of document certifying incorporation, establishment or registration of institution, documents that mention name, surname, address, profession, business of BOD and executive director r propreitor of firm or partners of partnership firm.
  3. In case of business relation or transactions to be established or made on behalf of someone else documents relating to principal's identity, address including power of attorney clarifying business of principal.
  4. Name, surname, address of sloe relative, person or institution benefiting from business transaction.
  5. In case of transactions made through negotiable instruments name, surname and address of issuer and payee of such instrument.
  6. Other documents as prescribed by FIU from time to time.

Bank and financial institution or non financial institution shall maintain separate record of documents, transactions of each customer including date, nature of transaction, type of account and code number.

Liability of Government entity, Bank and Financial Institution and Non financial Institution

The government entity, bank and finacial institution and non financial institution shall fulfill following repsonsibility for the purpose of this act.

  1. To maintain record of amount transacted beyond threshold prescribed by NRB at a single or series of transactions by a person.
  2. To investigate and inquire any transaction which appears to be suspicious or transacted with motive of asset laundering or so laundered or there are reasonable grounds for suspicion.
  3. To inform FIU about transaction mentioned in clause a within 15 days of event and transactions mentioned in clause b immediately after the event.

Incase there is any suspicion on transactions of person having regular business relation or transaction concerned entity, bank and financial institutions or non financial institution shall inform FIU after making inquiry.
